Creative Direction
Key Visuals

Liene
New York

A back-to-school activation for Liene at NYU Welcome, built around PixCut S1 and the idea of making memories you can hold.

At a busy welcome event, simply showing a printer was not enough. How do we make students stop, understand the product, and remember the brand?

We built the booth around a free NYU map and sticker kit. Students picked one up, personalised it, and watched PixCut S1 print and cut stickers live. Then they kept making it their own after the event, and shared it online for a chance to win one.

What students took home was exactly what the product could help them make.
